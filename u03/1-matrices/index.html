Idea · 6 min

A matrix and its shape

★ essentialMP

A matrix is a rectangular table of numbers. With mmm rows and nnn columns its shape is m×nm \times nm×n (“m by n”): rows first, as in df.shape. The number in row iii, column jjj is the entry aija_{ij}aij​. Maths counts from 1; NumPy counts from 0.

A matrix times a vector is a dot product for each row:

Ax=(row 1 of A⋅x⋮row m of A⋅x)\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} \text{row 1 of } \mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{x} \\ \vdots \\ \text{row } m \text{ of } \mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{x} \end{pmatrix}Ax=​row 1 of A⋅x⋮row m of A⋅x​​

So an m×nm \times nm×n matrix needs a vector with nnn components, and gives back mmm numbers.

Shapes. Each row of A\mathbf{A}A meets each column of B\mathbf{B}B in a dot product, so the inner numbers of the shapes must match; the outer ones give the result’s shape:

(m×n‾) (n‾×p)  →  m×p(m \times \underline{n})\,(\underline{n} \times p) \;\to\; m \times p(m×n​)(n​×p)→m×p

AB\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}AB is 3×33 \times 33×3; BA\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}BA is 4×24 \times 24×2
You kept the inner numbers. The result takes the outer numbers: rows of the first, columns of the second.

Tip · Transpose

A.T swaps rows and columns: row iii becomes column iii. The maths name is AT\mathbf{A}^\mathsf{T}AT. A 4×34 \times 34×3 matrix becomes 3×43 \times 43×4, so Q.T is another way to make shapes line up.

Wrap-up · 1 min

Wrap-up

You can now…

  • read a matrix’s shape m×nm \times nm×n and its entries aija_{ij}aij​, and find them in NumPy with A.shape and A[i, j], counting from 0;

  • decide from the shapes whether a product is defined and what shape it has.
