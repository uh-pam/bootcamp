Week 7 · Vectors, matrices, randomness

The ten-week plan

Week 7 of 10 Units: M8 · M9 · S5 · S6 With practice: ≈ 7.5 hours

Why this week

Linear algebra starts here. A row or a column of data becomes one object with an arithmetic of its own, and a matrix becomes an operation on it: the line you fitted in M6 comes back as a single multiplication that predicts the whole cohort. In Statistics, probabilities attach to numbers rather than events, which gives you expected values and variances, and then the standard distributions, each chosen by the mechanism that made the data rather than by the look of a histogram.

This week, in this order

Time
1 M8 · Data as vectors ≈ 1.5 h
2 M9 · Matrices as operations on data ≈ 2 h
3 S5 · Random variables ≈ 2 h
4 S6 · Distributions chosen by mechanism ≈ 2 h

Take them in this order. A unit may use anything above it in the list, and anything from an earlier week.

By the end of the week you can

  • add, scale and measure vectors, and compute a dot product and the angle it gives;
  • multiply matrices, check the shapes before you do, and read \(A\mathbf{x}\) as a prediction for every row;
  • compute an expected value and a variance, and use the rules for sums of random variables;
  • choose a distribution from how the data were generated, then check the choice against the data.
