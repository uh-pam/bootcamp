Scale matters

★ essentialMSP

A distance adds up squared differences, each in its column’s own units. Cluster penguins on bill depth (mm) and body mass (g):

In raw units, d(x,c1)=3.42+6502≈650.0d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{c}_1) = \sqrt{3.4^2 + 650^2} \approx 650.0d(x,c1​)=3.42+6502​≈650.0 and d(x,c2)=750d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{c}_2) = 750d(x,c2​)=750: grams swamp millimetres, and the bill depth hardly counts. The cure is to standardise each column to z-scores first (Lesson 3.4). A difference in z-scores is the raw difference divided by the column’s SD.

Wrap-up
