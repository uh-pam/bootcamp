cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ runs from 1 at 0°, through 0 at 90°, to −1 at 180°. The Maths refresh has a short section on sine and cosine.

Worked example: the angle between (3, 4) and (4, 3)
  1. Dot product: u⋅v=3×4+4×3=24\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 3 \times 4 + 4 \times 3 = 24u⋅v=3×4+4×3=24.
  2. Lengths: ∥u∥=∥v∥=9+16=5\|\mathbf{u}\| = \|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5∥u∥=∥v∥=9+16​=5.
  3. Divide: cos⁡θ=245×5=0.96\cos\theta = \dfrac{24}{5 \times 5} = 0.96cosθ=5×524​=0.96.
  4. Undo the cosine: θ=arccos⁡0.96≈16.3°\theta = \arccos 0.96 \approx 16.3°θ=arccos0.96≈16.3°.
