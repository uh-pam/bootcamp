It stays the same
Right. Every value and the mean both go up by 100 g, so every deviation xi−xˉx_i - \bar{x}xi​−xˉ is unchanged — and the SD is built only from the deviations.
It goes up by 100 g
That is what happens to the mean. The SD measures distances from the mean, and those do not change.

The SD measures spread, not size. Moving all values together leaves the spread exactly as it was.

Histograms: bins and counts

★ essentialSP

Here are all 333 flipper lengths as a histogram. Run it.

Python

Two humps: one near 190 mm (Adelie and Chinstrap) and one above 210 mm (Gentoo). Only 33 penguins are within 3 mm of the mean, 201 mm. A mean, median and SD could never show you that.

A histogram splits the number line into equal bins, counts the values in each bin, and draws a bar as tall as the count: a frequency table (Unit 1) for a numerical column.

Take eight flipper lengths, in mm, and bins 10 mm wide:

The bin “180 up to 190” holds 181 and 186, so its bar has height 2. A value on an edge, like 190, goes into the bin that starts there.

Worked example · 7 min

Box plots and the 1.5 × IQR rule

★ essentialMSP

A box plot draws the quartiles from Lesson 2.3:

  • the box runs from Q1Q_1Q1​ to Q3Q_3Q3​, so its height is the IQR;
  • the line inside the box is the median;
  • the whiskers reach out to the furthest values inside the fences;
  • dots beyond the whiskers are possible outliers.

A value xxx is flagged as an outlier when it lies beyond a fence:

x<Q1−1.5×IQRorx>Q3+1.5×IQRx < Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR} \quad \text{or} \quad x > Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR}x<Q1​−1.5×IQRorx>Q3​+1.5×IQR
Worked example: outliers among Chinstrap body masses

For the 68 Chinstrap penguins, Q1=3487.5Q_1 = 3487.5Q1​=3487.5 g and Q3=3950Q_3 = 3950Q3​=3950 g.

  1. IQR=3950−3487.5=462.5\text{IQR} = 3950 - 3487.5 = 462.5IQR=3950−3487.5=462.5 g.
  2. 1.5×IQR=693.751.5 \times \text{IQR} = 693.751.5×IQR=693.75 g.
  3. Lower fence: 3487.5−693.75=2793.753487.5 - 693.75 = 2793.753487.5−693.75=2793.75 g. Upper fence: 3950+693.75=4643.753950 + 693.75 = 4643.753950+693.75=4643.75 g.
  4. The lightest Chinstrap (2700 g) is below the lower fence and the heaviest (4800 g) is above the upper one: both are flagged.

Use it · 5 min

Describing shape

★ essentialS

Four words cover most shapes. Run the cell to see each one, in made-up data:

Python
  • Symmetric: the two sides are roughly mirror images (here, around one peak). Mean ≈ median.
  • Right-skewed: a long tail towards large values (incomes, house prices). The mean is pulled towards the tail, above the median.
  • Left-skewed: a long tail towards small values (marks on an easy test). The mean is below the median.
  • Bimodal: two peaks, often two groups mixed together, like the penguin flippers.

To describe a distribution, give its centre, spread, shape and outliers: “Flipper length is bimodal, with one peak near 190 mm and another above 210 mm; the median is 197 mm, the IQR is 23 mm, and there are no outliers.”
