x = np.array([5, 8, 2, 9]). What does x[1:3] give?

array([5, 8, 2])
Positions start at 0, so position 1 is 8, not 5. And the stop position, 3, is left out.
array([8, 2])
A slice starts at position 1 (8) and stops before position 3, so you get positions 1 and 2.
array([8, 2, 9])
The stop position is not included: 1:3 means positions 1 and 2 only.
array([5, 8])
That counts positions from 1. Python counts from 0, so x[1] is the second value, 8.
