Idea · 1 min

One path, twelve units

Bootcamp teaches the maths, statistics and Python you need for the MSc Data Science as one subject, a week at a time. It prepares you for what Fundamentals of Data Science (FDS), Data Handling and Visualisation (DHV) and Applied Data Science 1 (ADS1) do that week.

There is one path with twelve stops:

UnitWhenWhat it is
0 · Start herebefore term, or week 1Get set up, see where you are starting from, write your first Python. About 2 hours.
1–10one a week, from Wednesday 7 OctoberFour short lessons (three in the two busiest weeks), a unit check, a live tutorial and a Canvas quiz. About 4 hours a week.
11 · Bring it togetherweek 12One real question answered end to end. About 2 hours.

Nothing is locked. Every unit is open from day one, so you can work ahead whenever you have time.

Idea · 2 min

A week in Bootcamp

★ essential

Each unit runs from Wednesday to the following Tuesday, a few days before the module sessions it prepares you for.

DayWhat
WednesdayThe new unit becomes “This week” on the home page.
Wednesday → MondayWork through the four lessons (about 35 minutes each) and the unit check.
Tuesday, 16:00Tutorial on MS Teams: maths and statistics worked live from slides. It builds on the lessons, so do them first.
SundayAim to have that unit’s Canvas quiz done.

For Unit 1, that means: it opens Wednesday 7 October, its tutorial is Tuesday 13 October at 16:00, and its quiz is due Sunday 18 October.

Unit 0 has no tutorial and no Canvas quiz.

Idea · 2 min

How a lesson works

A lesson takes about 35 minutes, in steps of a few minutes. Each step ends with something to do: a question, a prediction or a few lines of Python.

  • Pause any time. Progress saves after each step, in this browser only; see My progress data to move it to another device.
  • Three lenses. Each key idea appears as a picture or data, as symbols (the maths) and as code (Python), side by side.
  • ★ essential marks the steps on the catch-up route. If you fall behind, do those first.
  • Unlimited tries. Every wrong answer gets an explanation, and hints and a worked solution are there when you need them.

Use it · 2 min

Plan your Bootcamp hours

★ essential

A week of Bootcamp takes about 4 hours. The people who keep going are rarely the most motivated: they decided in advance when. So decide now.

PartTime a weekExample slot
Lessons and unit checkabout 2 h 40Wednesday 18:00–19:30 and Saturday 10:00–11:30
Tutorial1 hTuesday 16:00–17:00 (fixed)
Canvas quizabout 30 minTuesday 17:00–17:30, straight after the tutorial

Your slots will differ. Make them specific: a day, a time and a place. Then say it as a plan: “On Wednesday at 18:00, at my desk, I open Bootcamp and do the next lesson.”

Now open your calendar and add:

  1. your two weekly Bootcamp slots, repeating until the week of 11 January;
  2. the tutorial, Tuesdays at 16:00, from 13 October.
Choose0a

Which plan is most likely to happen?

“Wednesday 18:00–19:30 in the library, and Saturday 10:00–11:30 at home.”
Specific days, times and places. When Wednesday 18:00 comes, there is nothing left to decide.
“I’ll do Bootcamp whenever I have a free moment.”
Free moments rarely arrive in an MSc week. Without a set time, Bootcamp slides to “tomorrow”.
“The whole unit on Sunday night.”
One long session is harder to protect and remembers less than two shorter ones a few days apart. And the lessons should come before the Tuesday tutorial.

Wrap-up
