Idea · 6 min

A difference in standard errors

★ essentialMSP

H0H_0H0​ and H1H_1H1​ are as in Lesson 10.1. The test statistic is now the difference in means measured in standard errors:

t=xˉ1−xˉ2s12/n1+s22/n2t = \dfrac{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2}{\sqrt{s_1^2/n_1 + s_2^2/n_2}}t=s12​/n1​+s22​/n2​​xˉ1​−xˉ2​​

The bottom is the SE of the difference, built from each group’s s/ns/\sqrt{n}s/n​ (Lesson 7.2). Under H0H_0H0​, ttt follows a t distribution, which plays the part of the shuffle histogram: a big ∣t∣|t|∣t∣ lands in a tail, so p is small.

One test, three ways

t=0.8040.278≈2.90t = \dfrac{0.804}{0.278} \approx 2.90t=0.2780.804​≈2.90

P Code

Always pass equal_var=False: Welch’s t-test doesn’t assume equal spreads in the two groups. SciPy’s default, equal_var=True, is the older pooled test. The test assumes independent observations and roughly normal sample means, which the central limit theorem (Lesson 7.1) gives for moderate nnn.

Counts and the χ² test

★ essentialMSP

For two categorical variables, such as season and wet month (over 45 mm), Lesson 9.1 asked: are they independent? The χ² test of independence tests H0H_0H0​: independent, against H1H_1H1​: not.

If H0H_0H0​ were true, each cell would hold its expected count:

E=row total×column totalNE = \dfrac{\text{row total} \times \text{column total}}{N}E=Nrow total×column total​

For Autumn and wet, E=90×179/360=44.75E = 90 \times 179 / 360 = 44.75E=90×179/360=44.75, against 52 observed. The test statistic adds up the gaps over all cells:

χ2=∑(O−E)2E\chi^2 = \sum \dfrac{(O - E)^2}{E}χ2=∑E(O−E)2​

Autumn and wet gives (52−44.75)2/44.75=1.17(52 - 44.75)^2 / 44.75 = 1.17(52−44.75)2/44.75=1.17; the eight cells add up to χ2=12.03\chi^2 = 12.03χ2=12.03. The p-value is the area beyond 12.03 under the χ² distribution with (r−1)(c−1)=(4−1)(2−1)=3(r-1)(c-1) = (4-1)(2-1) = 3(r−1)(c−1)=(4−1)(2−1)=3 degrees of freedom (dof): p=0.0073p = 0.0073p=0.0073.
