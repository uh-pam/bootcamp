Idea · 6 min

The t interval

★ essentialMSP

Replace the 2 by a number t∗t^{*}t∗ from the t distribution. The 95% confidence interval for a mean is

xˉ±t∗ sn\bar{x} \pm t^{*}\,\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}xˉ±t∗n​s​

The part after ±\pm±, t∗ SEt^{*}\,\mathrm{SE}t∗SE, is the margin of error. For 95%, t∗t^{*}t∗ = stats.t.ppf(0.975, n - 1): 2.5% lies above it, with n−1n - 1n−1 degrees of freedom.

Sample size nnn51020301191000
t∗t^{*}t∗ = stats.t.ppf(0.975, n - 1)2.7762.2622.0932.0451.9801.962

Why bigger for small nnn: sss is itself estimated, and from a few values it can be well off, so t∗t^{*}t∗ widens the interval to allow for that. For large nnn, t∗t^{*}t∗ is close to 1.96.

Use it · 6 min

What 95% means

★ essentialSP

Back to Lesson 7.1: the 333 penguins are the population, so μ=4207.1\mu = 4207.1μ=4207.1 g is known. Take 100 samples of 20, and make a 95% interval from each.

Python

Each line is one sample’s interval; the black line is μ\muμ. The intervals move from sample to sample, and 5 of the 100 miss μ\muμ (dashed, with crosses at their ends).

That is what 95% describes: the method catches μ\muμ in about 95% of samples. For your one interval, say: “we are 95% confident that the mean flipper length of all Gentoos lies between 216.0 and 218.4 mm.”

Don’t sayWhy not
95% of Gentoos have flippers in the intervalSingle penguins vary far more: their middle 95% is about 208 to 230 mm
There is a 95% probability that μ\muμ is in this intervalμ\muμ is fixed: this interval contains it or not. The 95% belongs to the method
95% of sample means lie in the intervalSample means scatter around μ\muμ, not around this xˉ\bar{x}xˉ

The width, 2 t∗s/n2\,t^{*} s/\sqrt{n}2t∗s/n​, has two dials. More confidence needs a wider net: for 99%, t∗t^{*}t∗ = stats.t.ppf(0.995, n - 1) =2.618= 2.618=2.618 (n=119n = 119n=119). More data narrows it: 4 times the penguins halves the SE, so roughly halves the width.
