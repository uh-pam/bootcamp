Idea · 6 min

Shuffle the labels

★ essentialMSP

A test starts from two claims about the populations, here the climate behind each decade:

  • the null hypothesis H0H_0H0​: no difference, so the labels “1990s” and “2010s” don’t matter;
  • the alternative H1H_1H1​: there is a difference, in either direction (two-sided).

The test statistic measures the gap in the data: the difference in group means. If H0H_0H0​ is true, the labels are arbitrary, so shuffling them gives another difference that H0H_0H0​ could produce. Thousands of shuffles give the null distribution; the p-value is the share of it at least as extreme as the real difference.

Use it · 6 min

Reading a p-value honestly

★ essentialS

Choose a threshold before you look: the significance level α=0.05\alpha = 0.05α=0.05 is a convention, not a law.

  • p<αp < \alphap<α: the data would be surprising if H0H_0H0​ were true. That is evidence against H0H_0H0​: the difference is “statistically significant”.
  • p≥αp \ge \alphap≥α: no evidence of a difference. That is not proof that there is none.
