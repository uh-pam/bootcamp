Idea · 6 min

Bar area is proportion

★ essentialMSP

With density=True, each bar’s area is the share of the data in its bin:

height=proportion in the binbin widthsoheight×width=proportion\text{height} = \frac{\text{proportion in the bin}}{\text{bin width}} \qquad\text{so}\qquad \text{height} \times \text{width} = \text{proportion}height=bin widthproportion in the bin​soheight×width=proportion

The bars together hold all the data, so their total area is 1.

Area as a share, three ways
S Picture
M Symbols

The bin from 190 to 195 mm holds 60 of the 333 penguins:

  1. proportion: 60/333=0.18060 / 333 = 0.18060/333=0.180
  2. height: 0.180/5=0.0360.180 / 5 = 0.0360.180/5=0.036 per mm
  3. back again: area =0.036×5=0.180= 0.036 \times 5 = 0.180=0.036×5=0.180

A height is a proportion per mm; only areas are proportions. The share between aaa and bbb is the sum of height × width over the bars from aaa to bbb.

Wrap-up · 1 min

Wrap-up

You can now…

  • read a proportion from a density histogram as the area of its bars, height × width;
  • find a proportion under a density curve as an area: a rectangle, an integral, or the 68–95–99.7 rule;
  • summarise a table group by group with groupby, picking one column and applying one statistic or several.

Keep this pattern:

IdeaMaths / pandas
density histogrambar area = height × width = proportion; total area 1
density curveproportion between aaa and bbb = ∫abf(x) dx\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx∫ab​f(x)dx; total area 1
one statistic per groupdf.groupby("g")["col"].mean()
several statistics per groupdf.groupby("g")["col"].agg(["mean", "std", "count"])
