How Bootcamp works

Bootcamp teaches the maths, statistics and Python you need for the MSc Data Science — as one subject, in small steps, a week at a time. It runs alongside your first-semester modules (Fundamentals of Data Science, Data Handling and Visualisation, Applied Data Science 1) and gets you ready for what they do that week.

One path, twelve units

Section titled “One path, twelve units”
UnitWhenWhat it is
0 · Start herebefore term, or week 1Get set up, check where you’re starting from, write your first Python. About 2 hours.
1–10teaching weeks 2–11One learning unit a week: four short lessons (three in weeks 10–11), a unit check, a live tutorial and a Canvas quiz. About 4 hours a week in total.
11 · Bring it togetherweek 12One real question, answered end to end — and a look back at everything you can now do. About 2 hours.

Nothing is locked. If you want a head start, go at your own pace; every unit is open from day one.

Lessons you can pause

Section titled “Lessons you can pause”

Each lesson is split into steps of 5–8 minutes. Every step ends with something to do — a question, a prediction or a few lines of Python. When you finish a step your progress is saved, so you can stop at any point and pick up exactly where you left off.

Every idea is shown three ways: as a picture or data, as symbols (the maths) and as code (Python). You don’t need to install anything to start: Python runs right here in your browser.

Steps marked ★ essential form the catch-up route. If you fall behind, do those first.

Two kinds of quiz

Section titled “Two kinds of quiz”
  • Self-checks (on this site) are for learning. You get instant feedback, an explanation for every wrong answer and unlimited tries. They are not graded and nobody else sees them. The unit check at the end of each unit tells you, outcome by outcome, whether you are secure or should practise again.
  • Canvas quizzes (compulsory) — one for each of Units 1–10, in the Bootcamp module on Canvas. Each has 10–12 questions and 3 attempts, and the pass mark is 60%. You need to pass all ten to pass Bootcamp. They test the same skills as the self-checks, with different questions, so a secure unit check means you’re ready.

Python on your own computer

Section titled “Python on your own computer”

Your MSc modules also use Python on your own computer (Anaconda with Jupyter and Spyder). Unit 0 walks you through setting it up. For extra practice with Python basics, your Applied Data Science 1 module gives you free access to DataCamp; each Bootcamp unit suggests one or two DataCamp chapters as optional drill.
