Reading a log scale

★ essentialSP

Logs turn multiplying into adding: ln⁡(1000 e0.05t)=ln⁡1000+0.05t\ln(1000\,e^{0.05t}) = \ln 1000 + 0.05tln(1000e0.05t)=ln1000+0.05t, a straight line in ttt. A log axis uses this to straighten growth.

Python
Worked example: reading the log axis
  1. Spacing. On a log axis, equal distances up mean equal factors: 1,000 to 2,000 is as far as 2,000 to 4,000.
  2. Straight line. So equal times give equal factors: here × 1.65 every 10 years.
  3. Read it. The balance is about £2,000 after 14 years, so after another 14 it doubles again, to about £4,000 — not £3,000.

A straight line on a log axis means growth by a constant factor; on an ordinary axis, by a constant amount.
