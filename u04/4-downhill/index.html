Worked example · 7 min

Walking downhill

★ essentialMP

For the mean, algebra solves S′(c)=0S'(c) = 0S′(c)=0. Most models in machine learning have no such formula. Gradient descent finds the bottom by walking: start with a guess, look at the slope, and step the other way.

c←c−η S′(c)c \leftarrow c - \eta\, S'(c)c←c−ηS′(c)

A negative slope makes ccc bigger; a positive slope makes it smaller. The learning rate η\etaη (“eta”) sets the step size.

Worked example: three steps for 2, 4, 9, from c = 0 with η = 0.1
